## Design Context

### Users
QVAC Hackathon judges, privacy-conscious builders, and people who want to query private wellness or health-education notes locally. They need to see that sensitive documents stay on consumer hardware while answers remain source-grounded and safety-bounded.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, local-first. The interface should feel like an evidence desk: focused, legible, and quietly technical without becoming a generic dark developer dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined light interface with ink, warm paper, clinical green, and measured red safety accents. The product should read as a serious privacy tool rather than a marketing page. Dense operational surfaces are preferred over oversized hero panels.

### Design Principles
- Make local execution visible through status, latency, hardware, and trace evidence.
- Keep private documents and source citations central to the workflow.
- Use restrained color with clear safety states and accessible contrast.
- Favor compact, repeatable judge-demo workflows over ornamental layouts.
- Make medical boundaries explicit without turning the app into a wall of warnings.
