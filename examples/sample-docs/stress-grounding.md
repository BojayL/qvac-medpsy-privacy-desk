# Stress Grounding Notes

Grounding exercises are short practices that redirect attention to the present moment. Examples include naming five visible objects, feeling the feet on the floor, or taking slow breaths with a longer exhale.

Grounding is not a cure and does not replace therapy or emergency support. It can be a temporary self-regulation tool while someone decides what support to seek.

For urgent safety risk, local emergency services or crisis resources should be contacted immediately.
