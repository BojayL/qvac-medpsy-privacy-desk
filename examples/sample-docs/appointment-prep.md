# Appointment Preparation Notes

Before a wellness appointment, write down the main concerns in plain language, when they started, what makes them better or worse, and any patterns noticed over the last few weeks.

Bring a current medication and supplement list, relevant medical history, recent sleep changes, and major life events that may affect stress or mood.

Helpful questions for a clinician include: what warning signs should I watch for, what options are appropriate to discuss, what follow-up timing makes sense, and what should I do if symptoms suddenly worsen.

This note is educational and should be reviewed with a qualified professional.
