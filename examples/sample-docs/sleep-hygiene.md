# Sleep Hygiene Reference

Consistent wake time is often more reliable than trying to force an early bedtime. A wind-down routine can include dimmer lights, less stimulating media, and a repeated calming cue.

Caffeine, alcohol, late heavy meals, intense late exercise, and long naps can interfere with sleep for some people.

If sleep problems persist or are associated with severe mood changes, breathing problems, or safety concerns, a clinician should be consulted.
